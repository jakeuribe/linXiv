usage: linxiv paper [-h]
                    {get,delete,versions,repair,restore,hard-delete,search,remove-from-all-projects} ...

positional arguments:
  {get,delete,versions,repair,restore,hard-delete,search,remove-from-all-projects}
    get                 Get full details for a paper
    delete              Soft-delete a paper
    versions            List all stored versions of a paper
    repair              Overwrite paper metadata in-place
    restore             Restore a soft-deleted paper
    hard-delete         Permanently delete a paper
    search              Full-text search within local library
    remove-from-all-projects
                        Remove a paper from every project

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
